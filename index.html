About Jayant Academy

Jayant Academy is a lively English Medium, Co-Educational school located in a beautiful & peaceful campus near Shyam Mandir, Raxaul. We are for parents who want their children to be in a caring family environment with individual attention, where they will be taught to think and given an international perspective to their education. We encourage children to develop good study skills, recognize the importance of reading, and develop their understanding of countries around the world through their history and cultures.
